      SYS.09 · SYMPTOMSSTATUS: LISTENING

      Something feel off with your network?

      Pick anything that sounds familiar. It all stays in your browser until you send the contact form.

      diag/what-you-picked

      What it might mean

      Nothing picked yet. Choose a symptom and we'll explain what it might mean and what we'd check.

        Talk to us about what you picked
        The Wi-Fi drops in some rooms

        Might mean: access points in the wrong spots, interference, or tired equipment.

        We'd check: coverage room by room and how the access points are set up.

        PCs feel slower than they should

        Might mean: missing updates, a failing drive, or too much starting up at login.

        We'd check: update status, drive health and what runs when the PC starts.

        Nobody's sure the backups work

        Might mean: backups that quietly stopped, or never covered the files that matter.

        We'd check: when they last ran, what they cover, and whether a restore actually works.

        A former employee might still have access

        Might mean: accounts that were never switched off when someone left.

        We'd check: every account, who it belongs to and what it can reach.

        Strange pop-ups, or emails "from us" we didn't send

        Might mean: an email account someone else has signed in to, or unwanted software on a PC.

        We'd check: the affected accounts and machines, and recent sign-ins.

        The printer and the internet fight every morning

        Might mean: two devices trying to use the same network address, or a router that needs attention.

        We'd check: how devices get their addresses and how the router is set up.

        Nobody knows what's plugged into the network

        Might mean: nothing is wrong yet. It just means nobody has a list.

        We'd check: everything that's connected, then write it down for you.
