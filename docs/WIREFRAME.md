## Strategy

- **Business:** Blue Computer Services (BCS), Livermore, California 94551. Owner Randall Nerison, supporting small business networks since 2002.
- **Buyer:** the owner or office manager of a small or medium-sized business with no full-time IT person, who worries about what is happening on the network without knowing how to check. Home users are out of scope.
- **Main conversion:** request a network audit. It is BCS's own lead offer, it is low commitment, and it opens the door to managed IT.
- **Second conversion:** phone call, main line (925) 605-5605 or direct (925) 887-0812.
- **Main objection:** "Who is this small company, and can I trust them with my network?" The answer is more than 20 years of named, owner-led experience. BCS publishes no reviews, so the page must never fake any.
- **Message:** "Let us manage your network, so you don't have to." (BCS's own line.)
